## 1. Description of Service & Local VPN Operation

WLT-AdBlocker is an on-device network filtering application for Android:
- **Local Loopback Filtering:** The software utilizes Android's `VpnService` API to establish a strictly local VPN tunnel on your device.
- **No Remote Proxying:** Network packets are NOT routed to any remote proxy, server, or commercial VPN provider. All filtering occurs directly inside the local Go engine (`wlt-core`) running on your smartphone.
